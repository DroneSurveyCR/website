## Spotify Description

How do construction drone surveys actually save money on a Costa Rica build — and how do they catch contractor overbilling? In this episode we break down site monitoring from the air: earthwork volume verification to the cubic meter, monthly progress reports, cut-and-fill against design, and as-built documentation that municipalities and MOPT increasingly require.

**Topics covered:**
- Why Costa Rica construction needs drone surveys — mountainous earthmoving, remote sites, two rainy seasons, SETENA documentation
- Earthwork volume verification — the highest-ROI use, where owners routinely find 5–15% discrepancies vs contractor invoices
- A real Alajuela example: billed 18,500 m³, actual 16,200 m³, ~$22,000 discrepancy, survey cost $1,960, net save over $20,000
- Monthly progress monitoring for lenders, absentee owners, and dispute evidence
- Cut-and-fill against the design surface, delivered as a Civil 3D-ready GeoTIFF overlay
- As-built documentation for municipal and MOPT project sign-off
- The deliverable set: orthophoto, DTM, volume report, difference map, point cloud, accuracy report
- Accuracy: ±1–3 cm vertical with RTK GPS and LiDAR
- The workflow: DGAC airspace, ground control reoccupation, DJI Matrice 300 RTK flight, 72-hour delivery
- Pricing: from $1,000 up to 5 ha, +$80/ha, with monthly-program discounts down to $800/visit

If you're an owner, developer, contractor, or project manager building in Costa Rica, this episode explains the site data that gives you a single source of truth.
