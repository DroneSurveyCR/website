## Spotify Description

Guanacaste is a province of big land — sprawling cattle ranches, a booming Pacific coast, and the tourism infrastructure connecting Liberia to the Gold Coast. In this episode we get into why lidar drone surveys have become essential out here: ranch mapping, coastal real estate, resorts and solar farms, agriculture, and the Maritime Zone rules every coastal buyer needs to know.

**Topics covered:**
- Why big land breaks traditional surveying — a 200-hectare ranch takes a ground crew weeks vs. 1–2 days by lidar drone
- Cattle ranch mapping: 25cm contour lines, water and drainage mapping, boundary verification against the Registro Nacional, INDER land-use classification
- Real example: a 180-hectare ranch near Hojancha mapped in one flight, delivered with 50cm contours in 5 business days
- Coastal real estate near Tamarindo, Nosara, Flamingo, and Papagayo — boundary, slope, and flood analysis before you buy
- The Zona Marítimo Terrestre: why the 200m coastal setback can dramatically change your buildable area
- Tourism infrastructure: resorts, boutique hotels, access roads from Liberia to the Gold Coast, and solar farms in the dry lowlands
- Agriculture beyond cattle: melon, sugarcane, rice, and teak — irrigation, drainage, canopy penetration, SENARA compliance
- Guanacaste's dry season advantage (late November–April) for clear skies and better lidar ground penetration
- Coverage from Liberia and La Cruz to Nicoya, Tamarindo, Nosara, Sámara, and the Papagayo Peninsula
- Pricing: from $1,000 per 5 hectares, $80/ha after, with a $150–$300 travel fee from San José

If you own a ranch, a farm, a coastal lot, or a development site in Guanacaste, this episode explains the survey data that makes the decision for you.
