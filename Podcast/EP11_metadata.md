## Spotify Description

Drone survey or traditional ground survey in Costa Rica — which do you actually need? In this episode we run the head-to-head: how each method works, the truth about accuracy, why drones are 40–70% cheaper, the massive speed and safety gaps, and where a licensed topógrafo is still legally required. Plus the smart hybrid approach that combines both.

**Topics covered:**
- How each method works: a 2–5 person ground crew with a total station vs a DJI Matrice 300 RTK with Zenmuse L1 firing 240,000 pulses/second
- The accuracy myth-buster: total station ±1–3 cm at hundreds of points vs LiDAR drone ±2–5 cm at millions of points
- Cost: $1,000 up to 5 ha plus $80/ha — and why drones run 40–70% cheaper than ground crews
- A real Pérez Zeledón example: 40 ha forested finca quoted at $7,200 / 6 weeks vs $3,800 / 5 days by drone, with LiDAR reaching the ground under canopy
- Speed: a 10 ha cleared site in 30 minutes vs 2–4 days; forested land 10x faster
- Safety: removing crew exposure on steep, wet, snake-and-wasp terrain — and leaving the land untouched
- Where ground surveys still win: cadastral pins, the Registro Nacional stamp, under-structure measurement, single-point verification
- A clear decision guide for when to choose each method
- The smart hybrid approach: drone for 90%, topógrafo stamps the boundary pins, still 40%+ cheaper
- DGAC: drone surveying is fully legal in Costa Rica with registered aircraft and a licensed operator

If you own land, are building, or are about to buy property in Costa Rica, this episode tells you exactly which survey — or which combination — you actually need.
