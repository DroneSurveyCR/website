Drone Surveys for Environmental Impact Assessments in Costa Rica — SETENA Data & 72-Hour Delivery

**Description:**
If you're developing land in Costa Rica, a SETENA Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) is a legal prerequisite — and the spatial data it requires is more detailed than most developers expect. In this episode we break down exactly what SETENA needs from a drone survey: georeferenced orthophotos, Digital Terrain Models, slope hazard maps, drainage basin delineation, vegetation coverage maps, and property boundary surveys. We explain why LiDAR is essential for forested properties, walk through the step-by-step survey process from DGAC flight registration to SETENA-ready deliverable package, and cover post-approval compliance monitoring. Transparent 2026 pricing included.

Topics covered:
• SETENA EIA categories (A, B1, B2, C) and who needs what
• The 6 spatial datasets SETENA requires for a complete EIA filing
• Why photogrammetry fails on vegetated terrain — and how LiDAR solves it
• Day-by-day survey process from mobilization to 72-hour delivery
• Post-approval compliance monitoring obligations and drone solutions
• 2026 pricing: $1,000 for up to 5 ha, $80/ha above that
