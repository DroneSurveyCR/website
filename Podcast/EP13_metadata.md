## Spotify Description

More than half of Costa Rica lives in the Central Valley — and every new building, subdivision, and road starts with the same terrain question. In this episode we tour the Valle Central province by province: free trade zones in Alajuela, agricultural slopes in Cartago, coffee-land conversion in Heredia, and how drone lidar powers construction from pre-design through final as-built.

**Topics covered:**
- Why the Central Valley drives drone survey demand — 3M+ people, 900–1,400m elevation, constant construction
- Alajuela: free trade zones along Ruta 27 and General Cañas, plus residential booms in Grecia, Atenas, and San Ramón
- Cartago: agricultural modernization on the slopes of Llano Grande and Pacayas, urban growth around Tres Ríos and Paraíso
- Heredia: coffee plantations converting to housing in Barva, San Rafael, and Santo Domingo; UltraPark and Global Park zonas francas
- The three construction phases: pre-construction DTM into Civil 3D, earthwork verification during build, as-built for occupancy permits
- Cut-and-fill volume estimates as the buy/no-buy decision lever for developers
- Independent monthly measurement vs. contractor self-reported earthwork numbers
- Infrastructure: lidar mapping a 15–20 km road or utility corridor in a single day
- Airspace: DGAC coordination near Juan Santamaría and Tobías Bolaños airports, handled as standard service
- Pricing: from $1,000 per 5 hectares, $80/ha after, and $0 travel fee for most sites within 90 minutes of San José

If you're building, developing, or buying land in Alajuela, Cartago, or Heredia, this episode explains the survey data your project actually needs.
