## SOURCE MATERIAL

### The Problem: Buying Property in Costa Rica Without a Drone Survey

Costa Rica is one of the most attractive places in the world to buy property — beaches, jungle, mountains, and a stable democracy. But it's also one of the trickiest legal environments for real estate. Land titles are often murky, boundary markers are unreliable, and what you see on a walkthrough may not match what you're actually buying.

Traditional property inspections miss critical details:
- True property boundaries, especially on irregular or sloped terrain
- Encroachments from neighbors (fences, roads, structures)
- Hidden waterways, creeks, and drainage issues
- Buildable vs. unbuildable area due to slope or setbacks
- Dense vegetation covering ground features
- Illegal structures or uses on the property

### How Drone Surveys Change Everything

A drone survey provides centimeter-accurate aerial mapping of an entire property in hours instead of days. Using a combination of high-resolution photogrammetry and LiDAR, DroneSurveyCR creates:

- **Orthomosaic maps** — a bird's-eye-view of the property with accurate dimensions
- **3D terrain models** — showing exact elevation, slope, and contours
- **Volumetric analysis** — how much soil, fill, or material is on the land
- **Boundary overlays** — comparing the legal plano catastrado with physical reality
- **Vegetation layers** — what's under the canopy using multi-return LiDAR

### Who Benefits

**Buyers:**
- See exactly what you're buying before signing
- Identify boundary disputes and encroachments upfront
- Understand buildable area for future construction
- Catch drainage and flooding issues before closing
- Get documentation for your lawyer and bank

**Sellers:**
- Prove the value of your property with visual data
- Show buildable potential to developers
- Resolve neighbor disputes with authoritative data
- Speed up closing by pre-answering buyer questions

**Developers:**
- Plan subdivisions with precise topographic data
- Calculate earthworks and site prep costs accurately
- Minimize surprises during construction
- Comply with MINAE, SETENA, and municipal requirements

### Real-World Examples

Example 1: A US buyer was about to close on a 4-hectare property in Guanacaste. A drone survey revealed the actual fenced area was 30% smaller than the plano — a neighbor had moved a fence 15 years ago and no one had noticed. The buyer negotiated a $40,000 price reduction.

Example 2: A developer in the Central Valley planned a 20-lot subdivision. LiDAR showed a hidden ravine running through three proposed lots. Redesigning before breaking ground saved them six figures in rework.

Example 3: A seller in Nosara couldn't understand why their beachfront lot wasn't selling. A drone survey showed exactly how much of the lot was above the 50-meter maritime zone — unlocking the listing and closing in 45 days.
