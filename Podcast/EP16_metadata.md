## Spotify Description

Costa Rica's South Pacific is where two of the toughest surveying challenges collide — 300-plus inches of rain a year and steep, forested terrain. In this episode we break down why drone surveys are essential for pineapple growers fighting drainage, buyers verifying steep-lot boundaries, and developers siting eco-lodges on the Osa Peninsula.

**Topics covered:**
- The region from Uvita and Ojochal down the Costa Ballena coast to Puerto Jiménez on the Osa Peninsula
- Pineapple drainage planning: 300+ inches of rain, waterlogging, and the lidar elevation model that shows exactly where water pools
- A real Ojochal case study: a 140-hectare farm where two new drainage ditches cut disease losses 35% and paid back the $1,800 survey in one harvest
- Why decades-old paper boundaries fail on steep forested terrain, and how orthomosaics reveal a property's true extent
- Coastal development risk: flagging flood, landslide, and drainage zones before building near Uvita, Ojochal, and Puerto Jiménez
- The Osa Peninsula eco-resort spotlight: lidar under dense canopy with the DJI Zenmuse L1 at 5 cm vertical accuracy
- Surveying near national parks like Marino Ballena — what's possible and what needs authorization
- Crop health monitoring from orthomosaics, RGB and multispectral, delivered in GIS formats for ArcGIS and QGIS
- MINAE and INVU permitting in Puntarenas, plus rainy-season timing and pricing from $1,000 per 5 hectares

If you farm, buy, or develop land in Costa Rica's Southern Zone, this episode explains the terrain data that protects your investment where the land doesn't forgive guesswork.
