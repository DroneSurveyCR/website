## Spotify Description

San José and the Greater Metropolitan Area are the busiest construction and development zone in Costa Rica — and a drone survey is the fastest, most accurate way to get the data those projects need. In this episode we cover GAM coverage with no travel surcharge, the five main ways people use drone surveys here, and why the valley's hilly, vegetated terrain often makes lidar the smart call.

**Topics covered:**
- Where we work in the GAM: San José city, Escazú, Santa Ana, Desamparados, Alajuelita, La Uruca, Moravia, Curridabat, Tres Ríos and more — no travel surcharge, next-business-day possible
- Construction monitoring: cut-and-fill volumes, earthwork progress, and defensible data for contractor disputes
- Topographic surveys for building permits — SETENA and municipal requirements, with DXF, GeoTIFF and contour deliverables
- Subdivision and cadastral mapping in partnership with licensed topographers
- Road and infrastructure projects, including the Ruta 27 corridor
- Commercial real estate assessment: slope, drainage, and buildable area before you buy
- The GAM terrain challenge — a 1,100 m valley ringed by hills, and why slopes vary so much
- Why lidar makes sense here: penetrating secondary vegetation to reach bare earth vs photogrammetry for flat open sites
- Timelines: quote-to-flight 1–3 business days, flight-to-data 3–5 business days, rush available
- Pricing: from $1,000 for sites up to 5 hectares, with no travel fees anywhere in the metro area

If you're building, subdividing, or buying land anywhere in San José or the wider GAM, this episode explains the survey data your project actually needs.
