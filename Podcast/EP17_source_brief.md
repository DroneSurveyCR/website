## SOURCE MATERIAL (Conversational Podcast-Ready Tone)

Picture the drive across the Central Valley on any given Tuesday. You've got a condo tower going up in Escazú, a road project tearing through Desamparados, an industrial park taking shape in La Uruca, and hillside houses stacking up the slopes of Moravia. San José and its surrounding cantons are, hands down, the most active construction and development zone in all of Costa Rica. There's always something being built, measured, or mapped. And for almost every one of those projects, the fastest, most accurate way to get the data you need is a drone survey. So today we're getting into what that actually looks like across the Greater Metropolitan Area — the GAM — and why the terrain here makes it such a perfect fit.

Let's start with geography, because it matters more than people think. The GAM covers a huge spread — from the dense urban center of San José city, out west through the corridor to Escazú and Santa Ana, south through Desamparados and Alajuelita, and east toward Curridabat and Tres Ríos. Throw in Moravia, Tibás, Goicoechea, La Uruca, Coronado — that's the zone. And here's the part that matters for your budget: because we're based right in San José, every one of those projects comes with no travel surcharge and fast turnaround. Most sites, we can be on the ground the next business day. No two-hour drive, no travel fee tacked onto the quote.

Now, what do people actually hire us for in this area? The big one is construction monitoring. Developers and project managers use drone surveys to track earthwork progress, calculate cut-and-fill volumes, and confirm the site matches the approved plans. One flight gives you a complete picture of where the project stands — way more useful than a tape measure, and a lot more defensible if there's ever a dispute with your contractor over how much dirt got moved.

The second big one is topographic surveys for building permits. SETENA and the municipal offices in San José require topographic surveys for most new construction. And the key thing is our deliverables — contour maps, DXF files, GeoTIFFs — come formatted to drop straight into your architect's or engineer's software. You get everything you need for the permit submission, in the right format, the first time.

Third, subdivision and cadastral mapping. If you're dividing a property, our aerial surveys give you accurate boundary data and elevation models that your land surveyor uses to finalize the subdivision plan. And this is important — we work alongside licensed topographers all the time. We handle the aerial data collection; they handle the legal registration. It's a partnership, not a replacement.

Fourth, road and infrastructure. Road design, drainage planning, retaining wall placement — all of it needs accurate elevation data. The Ruta 27 corridor and the roads feeding into it have seen steady infrastructure investment, and we've mapped dozens of projects in that area alone.

And fifth, commercial real estate assessment. Before you buy land in Escazú or Santa Ana, a drone survey tells you exactly what you're dealing with — slope percentages, drainage patterns, real buildable area. It's a small investment that can save you from a very expensive mistake.

Now here's the technical heart of it, and it's specific to this region: the GAM terrain challenge. San José sits in a valley at about 1,100 meters elevation, ringed by hills on every side. The land drops sharply toward the south around Alajuelita and rises steeply to the east in Moravia and Coronado. A flat lot in Santa Ana behaves completely differently from a hillside condo site in Escazú or a steep road cut in Desamparados. Traditional surveying methods really struggle with that variety. A drone with lidar handles it in a fraction of the time.

And that's exactly when lidar earns its keep. A lot of the hillside land you run into here has secondary vegetation — scrub, trees, overgrown grass — that blocks photogrammetry from ever seeing the actual ground surface. Lidar's laser pulses pass through the gaps in that vegetation and return data from the bare earth below. So for hillside construction sites or any property with real tree cover, lidar gives you the ground truth that photogrammetry simply can't. Now — for flat, open sites, like parking lots, cleared construction pads, or ag fields, photogrammetry works great and costs less. We'll tell you honestly which method your project actually needs when you request a quote. We're not going to upsell you into lidar you don't need.

Let's talk speed, because in construction, timing is everything. For San José area projects, quote to flight is usually one to three business days, depending on site access and your schedule. Flight to processed data is three to five business days for standard projects. And rush delivery is available — just ask when you request your quote. We know permit timelines and construction schedules don't wait around, so if you're on a deadline, tell us, and we'll do what we can to make it work.

A question we get a lot: can you survey in densely built urban areas? Yes. Our lidar system handles urban environments well — tight lots, slopes, areas with existing buildings, all manageable. For the really dense urban zones we coordinate with DGAC for any necessary clearances and adjust the flight plan accordingly. That's part of the service.

And pricing — let's keep it simple. It starts at $1,000 USD for sites up to 5 hectares, and there are no travel fees anywhere in the metropolitan area. If your project happens to fall outside the GAM — out in Guanacaste, Alajuela, the Caribbean coast — we still get there; travel fees apply for anything more than roughly two hours from San José, and we put the exact amount in your quote so there are zero surprises.

So if you've got a project anywhere in San José or the wider GAM — a building permit topo, a construction site to monitor, a property to assess before you buy — this is the data that moves it forward. Head to dronesurveycr.com/quote and we'll send you a quote within one business day, or message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you exactly which approach fits your site.
