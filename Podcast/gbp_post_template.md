**Purpose:** Cross-link each weekly blog + podcast episode to Google Business Profile for local SEO + discoverability in the Google knowledge panel.

**Frequency:** Once per week (Wednesdays, after podcast publish)
**Character limit:** 1500
**Media:** Always attach 1 image (drone photo, blog hero, or podcast cover art)
**CTA note:** GBP's streamlined composer has no separate button field — put the link inline

## Manual Posting Workflow

1. Go to Google Search → search "drone survey costa rica"
2. In the Business Profile panel → scroll to "Your business on Google" → click **Posts**
3. Click **+ Add post** → make sure "Update" type is selected
4. Paste the filled template into Description
5. **Attach an image** (drone photo, blog hero image, or podcast cover art)
6. Click **Post**

## Scheduling
GBP allows scheduled posts — toggle "Schedule this post" and pick a future date/time. Good for batching a month of posts in one session.
