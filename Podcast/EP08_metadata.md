## Spotify Description

How do LiDAR drones map the ground under dense jungle canopy? In this episode, we break down the physics of canopy penetration — how laser pulses pass through gaps in tropical forest to reveal the true terrain underneath — and why it matters for anyone buying, developing, or surveying forested land in Costa Rica.

**Topics covered:**
- Why camera drones fail in tropical forest — the canopy problem explained
- How LiDAR laser pulses physically penetrate vegetation gaps to reach the ground
- Multiple returns per pulse: how one laser beam maps canopy AND terrain simultaneously
- Canopy penetration rates by Costa Rica forest type (3–35% depending on density)
- What you get: bare-earth DTM, canopy height model, 3D point cloud, contour lines, slope maps
- Regional spotlight: Osa Peninsula, Caribbean coast, Central Valley, Guanacaste dry season advantage
- LiDAR vs photogrammetry: the 30% tree cover decision framework
- Accuracy under canopy: ±3–10 cm vs satellites at ±5–10 meters
- Cost breakdown: 20–30% LiDAR premium, and why it's cheaper than machete crews
- Zero environmental impact: completely non-contact survey method

If you're looking at forested property in Costa Rica — whether for development, conservation, or investment — this episode explains exactly what LiDAR reveals that no other technology can.
