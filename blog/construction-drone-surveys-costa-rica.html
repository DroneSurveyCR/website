Key Applications for Construction in Costa Rica

Pre-Construction Topographic Survey

Before a single shovel hits the ground, you need to understand your terrain. A drone topographic survey gives engineers and architects the accurate 3D data they need for grading plans, drainage design, road layout, and building placement. Costa Rica's mountainous terrain makes this especially critical — an error in your base topo can cascade into expensive rework during earthmoving.

We deliver contour maps at 0.5m or 1m intervals, Digital Elevation Models, and CAD-ready files your engineering team can import directly into AutoCAD, Civil 3D, or Revit.

Earthwork Volume Calculations

Cut and fill calculations are one of the biggest sources of disputes and cost overruns in Costa Rica construction. Traditional methods estimate volumes with a handful of cross-sections — drone surveys calculate volumes across the entire site with centimeter-level accuracy.

Compare pre- and post-excavation surfaces to get exact cut/fill volumes for contractor billing, dispute resolution, and project tracking. Our volume reports are accepted by engineers, banks, and government entities.

Progress Monitoring & Documentation

Schedule monthly, weekly, or milestone-based drone flights to track construction progress. Overlay current conditions against your design plans to identify deviations early — before they become expensive problems. Build a time-lapse documentation record for investor reporting, insurance claims, or permit compliance.

Remote investors and international clients especially appreciate regular drone progress reports — it gives them confidence without requiring site visits.

Stockpile Volume Measurement

Track aggregate, gravel, soil, and material stockpiles with precision. Drone-based stockpile measurements are significantly more accurate than wheel-loader estimates and eliminate the safety risks of manual measurement on large piles.

As-Built Documentation

Document completed infrastructure — roads, retaining walls, drainage systems, foundations — with georeferenced accuracy. As-built drone surveys create a permanent record for permits, warranties, maintenance planning, and future development phases.

Infrastructure & Road Design

Planning access roads, water lines, or electrical infrastructure through Costa Rica's complex terrain? A drone survey provides the topographic foundation for alignment studies, gradient calculations, and cost estimation. Avoid expensive surprises by knowing your terrain before you design.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you survey an active construction site safely?

Yes. We coordinate flight timing to minimize interference with ground operations and follow all safety protocols. Most survey flights are completed in 2–4 hours with minimal impact on site work.

How accurate are the volume calculations?

With RTK GPS and properly placed ground control points, our volume calculations achieve ±1–3% accuracy, which is significantly better than traditional estimation methods.

Do you work with construction companies throughout Costa Rica?

Yes — we work on construction projects in all seven provinces. Travel fees apply for sites outside the Central Valley. For large projects requiring multiple site visits, we can arrange project-based pricing.

Can you provide ongoing monitoring throughout a project?

Absolutely. Many construction clients set up monthly or milestone-based survey contracts for the duration of their project. Repeat surveys can be discounted for long-term project agreements.
