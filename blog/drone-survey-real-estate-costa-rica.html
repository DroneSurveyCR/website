Why Real Estate Transactions in Costa Rica Need Accurate Surveys

Costa Rica's property records have a well-known challenge: the official plano catastro (cadastral map) on file is often outdated, inaccurate, or doesn't match the physical boundaries on the ground. Properties registered decades ago were often measured with basic tools, and discrepancies of 10–30% between recorded and actual land area are common — especially in rural and mountain areas.

For buyers, this creates real risk. You could be purchasing a 10-hectare farm that actually measures 7 hectares. Or buying a lot where the boundaries overlap a neighboring property or a protected area. A current drone survey eliminates this uncertainty before you sign.

⚠️ Common Issue in Costa Rica

Many properties have registered boundaries that don't match GPS coordinates or physical markers on the ground. Always commission an independent survey before finalizing a purchase — especially for rural land over 1 hectare.

How Drone Surveys Help Real Estate in Costa Rica

🏡 Pre-Purchase Due Diligence

Verify the actual size, shape, and boundaries of a property before buying. Compare GPS-accurate boundaries against the registered plano catastro. Identify encroachments, easements, or protected zone overlaps before they become your problem.

📐 Plano Catastro & Boundary Surveys

GPS-accurate boundary surveys to create or update the official plano catastro for REGISTRO NACIONAL registration. Required for subdivision, lot sales, and mortgage financing. Our surveys meet the accuracy requirements for Costa Rican land registration.

🏗️ Development & Subdivision Planning

Topographic maps and 3D terrain models for architects, engineers, and developers planning roads, buildings, water systems, and lot subdivisions. Understand slopes, drainage patterns, and buildable areas before breaking ground.

📸 Aerial Photography & Marketing

Stunning 4K aerial photos and videos that showcase the property, surrounding views, ocean or mountain proximity, and access roads. International buyers expect professional aerial content — properties with drone imagery sell faster and at higher prices.

🌲 Forested Property Mapping

LiDAR surveys that map the ground beneath forest canopy — essential for forested farms, jungle properties, and land with FONAFIFO forest payments. Accurate area calculations for timber, carbon credits, and conservation easements.

What You Get from a Real Estate Drone Survey

Depending on your project needs, a complete drone survey package can include:

  • GPS-accurate boundary markers and coordinates — ready for your attorney and notary
  • Topographic contour maps — 0.5m or 1m interval contours for engineering and planning
  • High-resolution orthophoto — georeferenced aerial image of the entire property
  • 3D terrain model — visualize slopes, valleys, and buildable areas
  • Area calculations — total area, buildable area, slope classifications
  • CAD files — .dwg and .dxf format for architects and engineers
  • Professional aerial photos and video — for marketing and buyer presentations

How Long Does It Take?

Most residential and small commercial real estate surveys are completed within 3–5 business days from flight to final deliverable. Large farm or development projects may take 5–7 days. Rush turnaround is available for time-sensitive closings — contact us to discuss your timeline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a drone survey legally recognized in Costa Rica?

Yes. GPS-based boundary surveys produced by licensed professionals meet the requirements for the Registro Nacional. We provide data in the formats required for official registration and can work alongside your surveyor or topographer.

Can you survey land with no road access?

Yes. Drones can access remote properties that are difficult or impossible to reach on foot. As long as we can get our equipment to within flight range of the property, we can survey it.

Do you cover all provinces in Costa Rica?

Yes — we survey throughout Costa Rica including Guanacaste, Puntarenas, Limón, Cartago, Heredia, Alajuela, and San José. Travel fees apply for locations outside the Central Valley.

Can drone surveys be used for mortgage financing?

Drone survey data can support mortgage applications by providing accurate area calculations and boundary documentation. However, specific bank requirements vary — check with your lender about their survey requirements before ordering.
