South Pacific Drone Survey: Lidar Mapping for Pineapple Farms, Coastal Property & Land Development

Published April 12, 2026 5 min read

The South Pacific region of Costa Rica — from Uvita and Ojochal down the coast to Puerto Jiménez — is one of the country's most economically dynamic agricultural and real estate markets. Pineapple plantations dominate the landscape, while coastal property development and eco-tourism growth have accelerated land values. For agricultural producers and property owners in this region, precise terrain data is no longer optional; it's essential for competitiveness and risk management.

This guide explains why drone surveys are critical in South Pacific Costa Rica, what deliverables matter most for local land use, and how to get started with a free quote for your South Pacific property or farm.

Why South Pacific Farmers and Developers Need Drone Surveys

The South Pacific region is defined by complex terrain, tropical rainfall, and intensive agricultural use. These conditions create specific challenges that drone surveys solve:

1. Pineapple Plantation Drainage Planning

Pineapple is a high-value crop but extremely sensitive to poor drainage. The region receives 300+ inches of rain annually in some areas, and waterlogging causes root rot, nematode proliferation, and fungal disease. A lidar-derived elevation model shows exactly where water pools during heavy rains, allowing growers to design drainage systems, irrigation lines, and field rotation before planting. This prevents crop loss and extends plantation productivity.

2. Accurate Land Boundaries in Complex Terrain

Many South Pacific properties were surveyed decades ago using paper maps and outdated equipment. The terrain is steep and forested, making traditional boundary marking unreliable. Aerial orthomosaics and boundary overlays reveal the true extent of your property — critical before purchasing, subdividing, or developing land.

3. Coastal Property Development Risk Assessment

Coastal development in Uvita, Ojochal, and Puerto Jiménez often involves steep slopes and proximity to rivers. A drone survey identifies flood-prone areas, landslide risk zones, and natural drainage patterns — information that shapes building placement and engineering cost estimates.

4. Crop Health Monitoring and Disease Detection

Orthomosaics (high-resolution aerial photos) from drone surveys reveal crop health variations invisible from ground level. Yellow or brown patches in a pineapple field often indicate disease pockets, nutrient deficiency, or drainage problems. Early detection allows targeted intervention, reducing losses.

Typical South Pacific Drone Survey Project: A Case Study

Scenario: A 140-hectare pineapple producer in Ojochal, Puntarenas, is planning a third planting cycle and needs to assess drainage and field rotation.

Deliverables:

  • Lidar-Derived Elevation Model — Digital elevation at 5 cm accuracy showing every slope and depression across the 140 hectares
  • Contour Map — 0.5-meter contour lines revealing drainage patterns and natural water flow
  • Orthomosaic — Full-color aerial photo mosaic at 2 cm per pixel, showing crop condition, existing drainage infrastructure, and field boundaries
  • 3D Terrain Model — Interactive terrain visualization for drainage design and field planning

Impact: Using the elevation data, the grower designed two new drainage ditches along natural valley lines, preventing waterlogging in low-lying fields. They rotated replant cycles to allow soil recovery, reducing disease pressure. Total cost: $1,800 (site size + travel fee). First-year disease losses dropped 35%, paying for the survey within the first harvest.

South Pacific Specific Considerations

Rainy Season Timing

May–November is the rainy season. Cloud cover and muddy conditions can delay surveys. We recommend scheduling large projects in the dry season (December–April) for best results, though we can work year-round with advance notice.

Pineapple-Specific Outputs

For pineapple farms, we prioritize:

  • Contour accuracy (within 10 cm elevation)
  • Orthomosaics at highest resolution (RGB and multispectral options available)
  • Delivered in GIS formats (GeoTIFF, shapefiles) for integration with farm management software

Regulatory Context

Land development in Puntarenas requires permits from MINAE (environmental ministry) and INVU (housing institute). A drone survey accelerates permitting by providing the environmental and topographic baseline required in applications. We provide certified deliverables suitable for official submissions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is a drone lidar survey in dense forest or pineapple canopy?

Our DJI Zenmuse L1 lidar penetrates through foliage much better than optical methods, delivering 5 cm vertical accuracy even under dense canopy. Photogrammetry (standard aerial photos) works well for orthomosaics and visible crop condition but doesn't penetrate foliage; lidar gives you the ground elevation under the crop — exactly what you need for drainage planning.

Can you work with my existing GIS or farm management software?

Yes. We deliver lidar point clouds, orthomosaics, and boundary shapefiles in standard GIS formats (GeoTIFF, LAS, shapefiles) compatible with ArcGIS, QGIS, and most farm planning platforms. If you're unsure about format needs, tell us what software you use and we'll ensure compatibility.

What if my property straddles protected land or national park boundaries?

We can survey private land adjacent to national parks (e.g., properties near Marino Ballena) and deliver maps suitable for permit applications. We cannot survey within national parks themselves without special authorization. Let us know your location and we'll confirm feasibility during the quote process.
