Why LiDAR — Not Photogrammetry — in the South Pacific

The South Pacific and Osa Peninsula contain some of the most biodiverse and densely vegetated terrain in Costa Rica. Photogrammetry cameras capture what the sensor sees — which in primary rainforest means a canopy of leaves, not the ground. LiDAR pulses penetrate through gaps in the canopy and bounce off the forest floor, giving you accurate ground elevation data even under closed-canopy jungle.

Key advantage: Our LiDAR sensors emit up to 3 returns per laser pulse — the first return maps the canopy top, intermediate returns catch mid-story vegetation, and the last return maps bare earth. In conservation concession surveys and eco-resort site plans, this means you get a true terrain model regardless of vegetation density.

Frequently Asked Questions — South Pacific Drone Surveys

Can drones survey thick jungle canopy in the Osa Peninsula?

Yes. We use LiDAR sensors that emit up to 3 laser returns per pulse, penetrating dense rainforest canopy to map the ground surface below. This is essential in the Osa Peninsula where photogrammetry cameras simply cannot see through the vegetation.

Do you cover remote areas like Drake Bay or Corcovado buffer zones?

Yes. We mobilise ground teams and equipment to remote South Pacific locations including Drake Bay, Palmar Norte, Puerto Jiménez, and properties bordering Corcovado National Park. Logistics are factored into your quote.

What drone survey data does SETENA require for an EIA in the South Pacific?

SETENA typically requires topographic plans, hydrological flow maps, and accurate area calculations. Our LiDAR deliverables — DEM, DSM, contour lines, and orthophoto — meet all standard EIA submission requirements.

How long does a drone survey take in the South Pacific?

Flight operations typically take 1–2 days on-site depending on property size and access. Processed deliverables — DEM, DSM, contours, orthophoto, and full report — are delivered within 72 hours of flight completion.

What is the cost of a drone survey in the South Pacific?

Base pricing starts at $1,000 USD for up to 5 hectares, including flight, GPS base station, full data processing, and all deliverables. Properties beyond 5 ha are quoted at $80/ha. Remote access in areas like Drake Bay may include a logistics surcharge.
