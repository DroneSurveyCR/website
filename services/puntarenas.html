Drone Survey Services in Puntarenas Province

Coastal mapping, port infrastructure surveys, and agricultural LiDAR across Costa Rica's largest Pacific province — from Jacó and Manuel Antonio to the Osa Peninsula

Why Puntarenas Province Needs Professional Drone Surveys

Puntarenas is Costa Rica's largest province by area, spanning the entire central and southern Pacific coast from the Gulf of Nicoya down to the Panamanian border. This vast territory contains some of the country's most dynamic development zones alongside its most pristine wilderness, creating a diverse range of surveying demands that drone technology is uniquely equipped to address.

The coastal corridor from Jacó through Quepos to Manuel Antonio ranks among Costa Rica's busiest tourism and real estate markets. Condominium towers, boutique hotels, and residential communities continue to multiply along this stretch of Pacific coastline, each project requiring precise topographic data for site design, drainage engineering, and compliance with maritime zone regulations. Farther south, the Dominical and Uvita corridor attracts development on steep, forested hillsides where traditional ground surveys are impractical.

Puntarenas also hosts major industrial and agricultural operations that depend on accurate terrain data. The port of Caldera handles a significant share of Costa Rica's Pacific trade, and the palm oil plantations stretching through the Osa and Parrita districts cover thousands of hectares of flat and gently rolling terrain that requires drainage optimization and yield monitoring. Our LiDAR surveys provide the elevation models and vegetation analysis these operations need for efficient land management.

The province's southern reaches include the Osa Peninsula, home to Corcovado National Park and some of the most biodiverse rainforest on Earth. Environmental research, conservation monitoring, and ecotourism development in this region all benefit from high-resolution aerial data that only drone surveys can provide efficiently in such remote, roadless terrain.

Puntarenas Province Use Cases

Hillside Resort Development

The terrain between Jacó and Manuel Antonio consists of steep, forested hills overlooking the Pacific. Developers building on these slopes need precise contour maps and cut-and-fill calculations that account for the actual ground surface beneath dense vegetation. Our LiDAR surveys penetrate canopy to deliver the terrain models that architects and civil engineers require for safe hillside construction.

Palm Oil Plantation Management

The lowland plains between Parrita and Palmar Sur contain extensive palm oil operations where proper drainage is critical to crop health and yield. Drone surveys generate detailed elevation models that plantation managers use to design and maintain drainage networks, identify waterlogged areas, and plan new planting blocks across large acreages efficiently.

Coastal Erosion & Shoreline Monitoring

Pacific storms and rising sea levels threaten beachfront properties and infrastructure throughout Puntarenas. Regular drone surveys quantify shoreline retreat, document sand movement patterns, and provide the baseline data needed for coastal protection engineering at vulnerable sites from Puntarenas city through Esterillos and south to Dominical.

Osa Peninsula Conservation

The Osa Peninsula's remote rainforests require monitoring for deforestation, wildlife corridors, and reforestation progress. LiDAR surveys map forest structure, estimate biomass, and track landscape changes over time without the need for ground crews to traverse some of the most challenging terrain in Central America.

Frequently Asked Questions — Drone Surveys in Puntarenas

Can you survey remote areas like the Osa Peninsula?

Yes. We regularly deploy to remote locations throughout Puntarenas province, including the Osa Peninsula, Drake Bay, and the Terraba-Sierpe wetlands. Our portable DJI Matrice 300 RTK system operates from any cleared launch site, and we plan logistics to cover multiple survey areas per mobilization to keep costs efficient for remote projects.

How do you handle the rainy season in Puntarenas?

Puntarenas receives heavy rainfall from May through November, but mornings are typically clear. We schedule flights during morning windows before afternoon convective storms develop. LiDAR also performs well in light rain and overcast conditions, giving us more operational flexibility than photogrammetry-only systems.

What deliverables do you provide for coastal development projects?

For coastal projects we deliver georeferenced topographic maps, digital elevation models showing terrain relative to sea level, contour maps in DWG format for engineering design, high-resolution orthophoto mosaics, and slope analysis maps. All data references Costa Rica's official coordinate system and is compatible with AutoCAD and GIS platforms.

Can drone surveys support palm oil plantation drainage design?

Absolutely. LiDAR elevation models with 25-centimeter resolution reveal micro-topography that determines water flow patterns across flat plantation terrain. Engineers use this data to design drainage channels, identify low points prone to waterlogging, and optimize the layout of new planting areas for maximum productivity.
