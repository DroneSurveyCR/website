About Drone Survey Costa Rica

Costa Rica's trusted partner for precision aerial surveying, LiDAR mapping, and drone photography

Who We Are

Drone Survey Costa Rica is a professional drone survey company based in San José, serving all of Costa Rica with cutting-edge aerial surveying solutions. We specialize in LiDAR mapping, photogrammetry, GPS surveys, aerial video and photography for a diverse range of industries.

Using state-of-the-art DJI Matrice 300 RTK with Zenmuse L1 LiDAR sensor, we deliver centimeter-accurate results that exceed industry standards. Our team combines technical expertise with extensive field experience to ensure every project is completed to the highest specifications.

Our Equipment

DJI Matrice 300 RTK

Our flagship platform offers exceptional payload capacity, extended flight time, and RTK positioning for survey-grade accuracy across large areas.

Zenmuse L1 LiDAR

Full-waveform LiDAR sensor for high-density point cloud generation, delivering centimeter-level accuracy for complex terrain mapping.

DJI D-RTK 2 Base Station

Mobile GNSS base station providing real-time kinematic corrections for survey-grade positioning anywhere in Costa Rica.

Zenmuse P1 Camera

45MP full-frame camera for ultra-high-resolution photogrammetry and precise orthomosaic generation.

DJI Mavic 3

Professional video and still photography platform with four-thirds sensor for premium aerial content creation.
