1. Services Offered

Drone Survey Costa Rica provides professional aerial surveying services throughout Costa Rica, including but not limited to:

  • LiDAR mapping: High-density point cloud generation using DJI Matrice 300 RTK with Zenmuse L1 LiDAR sensor for centimeter-accurate terrain models.
  • Photogrammetry: Orthomosaic maps, 3D models, and volumetric measurements from aerial imagery.
  • GPS geopositioning surveys: RTK-enabled ground control point placement and georeferencing.
  • Aerial video and photography: Professional drone footage for real estate, construction documentation, marketing, and inspections.

Services are available across all provinces of Costa Rica, including San José, Alajuela, Cartago, Heredia, Guanacaste, Puntarenas, and Limón.
